## Personalized orders (every sticker order)

Kiss-Cut stickers allow only 1 layer in Printify, so names can't be a Printify text layer.
Buyers type names in Etsy's required personalization box; with Printify **Order approval: Manual**,
each order waits. Send Claude the name(s), get the named print file, replace the design on that order
in Printify, then approve.

## Before you publish (Printify) — done

1. Open each draft. Add a **Personalization text layer** on the banner/name area, the same as the shirt:
   - Family Cruise: font **Anordighos**, navy `#1d3557`. Center it in the sunset circle (The / Smith / Family).
   - Anniversary: font **Hashed Browns**, white `#ffffff`, centered on the navy banner.
   - Girls Trip: font **Bobogie Groovy**, white, one layer per chair banner (Name 1 left, Name 2 right).
2. Check the preview shows **one** name, inside the banner.
3. Shipping: set the sticker shipping profile to **free shipping** (price already includes it).
4. Publish to Etsy. Tell me when, and I'll do steps A–D below.

## Pricing & packaging research (what sells vs. what makes the most)

What competing sellers do (Etsy listings and seller guides, Nov 2026; listing prices, not verified sales data):

| Format | Typical price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Single custom name sticker | **$2.39–$5** | Mostly self-printed; often free shipping |
| Cruise-themed personalized decal | **$3.50–$5** | Tumbler/cup decals |
| Packs of 3–5 | **$7–$12** | Called the most popular sticker format |
| "Pick any 10" bundle | **~$11, free shipping** | Highest review counts in the sticker category (86.8k) |
| Size | **3"** most popular | 4" for laptops, coolers, statement pieces |

**What this means for print-on-demand:** Printify's $4.59 first-sticker shipping means POD can't
win on cheap singles. Profit comes from **multiple stickers in one shipment**:

| Offer (free shipping) | Buyer pays | Your profit* |
|---|---|---|
| 1 × 3" personalized | $8.99 | ~$1.96 |
| each extra 3" in same order | +$8.99 | ~$6.71 each |
| Family of 4 × 3" | $35.96 | ~$22.10 |
| **Personalized 6-pack sheet** (Kiss-Cut Sticker Sheet 5.83"×8.27", ~$5.14 + $4.59 ship) | $16.99 | **~$5.20** |
| same sheet at $19.99 | $19.99 | **~$7.85** |

\*After Printify cost/shipping and Etsy fees ($0.20 listing + 6.5% transaction + ~3% + $0.25 processing).

**Recommended lineup**
1. **Lead product: "Personalized Crew Pack": 6 matching name stickers on one sheet, $16.99–$19.99 free shipping.**
   It matches the format buyers want (a pack), costs about $2.83–$3.33 per sticker (inside the market range),
   and ships as one item. Before launch, check in Printify that one personalization entry can fill all 6
   name spots on the sheet.
2. **Single 3"/4" stickers ($8.99/$10.99)** as the add-on to shirt orders, written to sell multiples
   ("one for every bag").
3. Turn on an **Etsy sale for orders of 3+ items** to push multi-sticker carts and get shirt + sticker
   orders over the $35 free-shipping threshold.
