# Cruise Group Shop: Launch Kit

**Products:** Personalized matching cruise-group shirts (print-on-demand through Printify) and printable or editable cabin door decoration kits (digital downloads).
**Sales channels:** Etsy first, with Shopify as a second storefront later.
**Goal:** 20 live listings within 72 hours, with no client calls and no inventory.

> **Note:** Prices, fees and production times below are approximate as of late 2026. Check current numbers in Printify and Etsy before setting final prices.

## 2. Legal ground rules (read before designing anything)

1. **Never use cruise line names, ship names, logos or ship profiles.** That includes Royal Caribbean, Carnival, Disney, Norwegian, MSC, Celebrity, Princess, Virgin and any named ship. Etsy removes infringing listings, and repeated strikes can close your shop. Use a generic cruise-ship silhouette only.
2. **Search every catchphrase at tmsearch.uspto.gov** for apparel registrations before listing it. Phrases like "Vitamin Sea" and "Ship Happens" may have apparel filings, so check them carefully and drop any that are registered for clothing.
3. **AI art:** use tools whose plan allows commercial use and print-on-demand resale (check Kittl, Higgsfield, Runway and the rest for your plan). Don't prompt for copyrighted characters or "in the style of [brand]".
4. **Editable Canva templates:** you may sell access to a Canva template link. Build templates from your own art and Canva Free or Pro elements, and deliver them as a template link only, never as exported Pro elements.

### B. Door decoration kits (digital download, no production costs)

**Important design rule:** cruise cabin doors are metal, and most cruise lines don't allow tape. Design everything to be printed on **printable magnet sheets** or held up with **magnetic hooks or clips**, and say so in every listing.

Each kit contains:
1. "Welcome Aboard" door banner, printed as 4–6 letter-size panels that join into one banner
2. A personalized family or group name sign
3. Name tags for each cabin member (6 per page)
4. A "Do Not Disturb, Napping at Sea" sign
5. A sailing-date and cabin-number sign
6. Two blank "note" signs for messages between cabins

Each kit comes in two formats:
- **Printable PDF:** US Letter and A4, at a fixed price with set wording
- **Editable Canva link:** buyers change names, dates and wording themselves. This is personalization with no work for you, and the most profitable product you'll sell.

**Three themes to launch with:** Retro Sunset, Tropical Palm, and Classic Nautical (navy and white).

### C. Bundles

- **Crew Bundle (digital):** all 3 door kits at a discount
- **Matching Set:** shirt design plus the matching door kit (sell the door kit as a separate listing with the same theme and cross-link the two)
- **Sail & Seek add-on:** include a one-page PDF in every digital download offering a discount code for the Sail & Seek scavenger-hunt app

## 5. Pricing sheet

**Etsy fees (approximate):** $0.20 per listing, 6.5% transaction fee, about 3% + $0.25 payment processing, and a 15% Offsite Ads fee only on sales that come from Etsy's own outside ads. Shops under $10k a year in sales can opt out of Offsite Ads; do that at the start.

**Shipping strategy:** US buyers filter for free shipping, and group orders almost always pass Etsy's $35 free-shipping threshold. So **include shipping in the price and offer free shipping.**

| Product | Approx. Printify cost + shipping | Your price | Approx. profit per item |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adult tee, Bella+Canvas 3001 | $12–14 + about $4.75 first item / $2.50 each extra | **$29.99** | about $10–13 per shirt in a group order |
| Budget adult tee, Gildan 64000 | $9–11 + shipping | **$24.99** | about $9–11 |
| Kids or toddler tee | $10–12 + shipping | **$24.99** | about $8–10 |
| Women's tank | $12–14 + shipping | **$29.99** | about $10–12 |
| Printable door kit (PDF) | $0 | **$9.99** | about $8.70 |
| Editable Canva door kit | $0 | **$14.99** | about $13.20 |
| Crew Bundle (all 3 door kits, editable) | $0 | **$29.99** | about $26.50 |
| Extra name sign or name tags | $0 | **$4.99** | about $4.20 |

**Group order discount:** use Etsy's built-in "buy 6+ items, get 10% off" promotion. It encourages buyers to order shirts for the whole group, and a 10-shirt order still makes about $90–$110 profit.

**Rush option:** add a "Rush Production +$12" listing for buyers sailing within 2 weeks, but only if your Printify provider can actually turn orders around faster.

**Launch costs (about $40–$80):**
- Etsy shop setup fee (one-time, if it applies to you): about $15

## 8. Etsy and Printify setup

1. **Open the Etsy shop** under Sail & Seek LLC and add your banking and tax details.
2. **Shop policies:** say all sales on personalized items are final; Printify reprints anything that arrives defective or misprinted; and digital downloads are non-refundable.
3. **Connect Printify to Etsy.** Set production to the US provider you chose and turn on Printify's Etsy order sync.
4. **Personalized orders:** Etsy personalization text reaches Printify with the order. You open each order and change the `[NAME]`/`[YEAR]` text layer before approving it for production, which takes about 3–5 minutes per order. **This is the only manual step.** When volume grows, consider a personalization app or moving to Shopify with a tool that updates the design automatically.
5. **Turn on the 6+ items, 10% off promotion.**
6. **Opt out of Offsite Ads** while you're under $10k in yearly sales, unless you want the outside traffic.
7. **Processing time:** set it to 3–5 business days so delivery promises are honest.

## 9. Getting buyers (no cold outreach)

- **Etsy search:** this is your main channel. More listings mean more chances to be found; aim for 20 at launch and 40 within a month. Each theme × each product type (tee, kids, tank, door kit) counts as a separate listing.
- **Pinterest:** cruise planners use it heavily. Make a business account and pin every listing image with keyword-rich pin titles. Pins keep bringing traffic for months.
- **Short videos (TikTok, Instagram Reels):** "Decorate our cabin door with me", "Matching cruise shirts for 12 people", and AI-made vacation scenes. Post 3 a week and link the Etsy shop in your bio.
- **Cruise Facebook groups:** people organize sailings in "roll call" groups, one per ship and date. **Most ban selling, so read each group's rules.** Where it's allowed, post a free printable "Do Not Disturb" sign that links to your shop.
- **Etsy Ads:** after you have 15+ listings and some favorites, try $1–$3 a day on your two best listings and check results weekly.
- **Sail & Seek cross-promotion:** add a "Matching shirts & door kits" link to your Sail & Seek website and funnel, and put the hunt discount in every door kit PDF.

**Seasonality:** booking season runs January–March, with many sailings planned for spring break and summer, so shirt orders rise from February to June. Black Friday through December is good for cruise **gift** angles, such as "Cruise reveal gift" shirts and "We're going on a cruise!" surprise printables. Consider adding a **cruise reveal printable** now; it's a popular holiday gift idea.

## 11. Weekly numbers to track

| Metric | Where to find it | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Views per listing | Etsy Stats | Whether search is finding you (if low, fix the title and tags) |
| Favorites per listing | Etsy Stats | Whether people like the design |
| Orders and average order size | Etsy Orders | Whether groups are buying in bulk |
| Conversion rate (orders ÷ visits) | Etsy Stats | Whether photos and price are working |
| Top traffic sources | Etsy Stats | Which channel to put more time into |

**Rules of thumb:**
- Views but no favorites: improve the design or the main photo.
- Favorites but no sales: look at price, shipping and the photos.
- No views at all: rewrite the title and tags.
