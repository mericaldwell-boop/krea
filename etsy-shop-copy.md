**Production partner** *(required by Etsy, under Shop Manager → Settings → Production partners)*:
- Name: **Printify** (or the print provider you choose in Printify)
- Description: *"Print-on-demand partner who prints and ships my original shirt designs."*
- Then on each shirt listing, under "Who made it?", choose **"Another company or person"** and select Printify as the production partner. Etsy requires this for print-on-demand items, and leaving it out can get listings removed. Etsy's listing form changes from time to time, so if these options look different, pick whatever says you designed the item and a production partner made it.
- Digital door kits are made entirely by you, so choose **"I did"** on those listings.

### FAQs
*Shop Manager → Settings → Policies → FAQs*

**How do I order matching shirts for my whole group?**
```
Add each shirt to your cart one at a time, choosing the size (and role, if applicable) and entering your personalization for each. Buy 6+ items and the 10% group discount applies automatically.
```

**What size should I order?**
```
Our adult shirts are unisex sizing, which runs slightly larger for women. Check the size chart photo in each listing. When in doubt, size down for a fitted look or stay true to size for a relaxed fit.
```

**Can I put tape on my cruise cabin door?**
```
Most cruise lines don't allow tape on cabin doors. Cabin doors are metal, so print your decorations on printable magnet sheets, or use magnetic hooks and clips. Check your cruise line's rules before you sail.
```

**Do I need Canva to edit the door kits?**
```
You only need a free Canva account. Open the link in your download, click "Use template," and edit names, dates, and text right in your browser or the Canva app.
```

**I'm sailing soon. Can you rush my order?**
```
Message us before ordering with your sailing date and we'll let you know what's possible. Digital door kits are instant, so those are always ready in time.
```

**Are these affiliated with a cruise line?**
```
No. All designs are original Sail & Seek creations and are not affiliated with or endorsed by any cruise line.
```
