# Cruise Sticker Line — Startup Walkthrough

How to add print-on-demand cruise stickers to Sail & Seek Studio using what you already have:
**Canva · Photopea · Printify · Etsy · Shopify · GoHighLevel (GHL)**.

You already run this exact workflow for shirts and magnets (design → Printify → Etsy). Stickers are the
same process with a smaller canvas, so most of this is reuse.

---

## 1. Why stickers fit your shop

- **Your buyers are already there.** Someone buying a Family Cruise shirt or a door magnet is the
  ideal sticker buyer: a cheap add-on for tumblers, laptops, luggage and cabin doors.
- **Matching sets sell.** Every shirt design you have can become a sticker in an evening, and the
  "match the whole crew" section you use on the door kit works the same way for stickers.
- **Low price, impulse buy.** Stickers sell on price and volume, so the money is in **bundles** and
  **add-ons**, not single stickers (see pricing, section 6).

Kiss-Cut sizes and print areas (Printify Choice, provider 99):

| Size | Print area |
|---|---|

**Design every sticker at 2000 × 2000 px.** One file then covers every size.

## 3. Design in Canva

1. **New design → Custom size 2000 × 2000 px.**
2. **Reuse your shirt art.** Open the existing design (Family Cruise sunset, Girls Trip chairs,
   Anniversary heart, Cruise Crew Loading bar) and copy it in. Most shirt designs need trimming:
   - Keep **one strong graphic and 2–6 words**, readable at 3" (a tumbler sticker).
   - Thin script and tiny names disappear at 2"–3". Thicken or enlarge them.
3. **Keep a margin.** Leave about **60 px of empty space on every side**. Kiss-cut trimming
   isn't pixel-perfect.
4. **Add a white "sticker border" (die-cut look).**
   - Duplicate the graphic, recolor the copy solid white, enlarge it slightly and put it behind.
   - Or do it cleanly in Photopea (step 4) — that version is better.
5. **Download:** PNG → **Transparent background** checked (Canva Pro) → size 1×.
   **Never** export JPG or take a screenshot.
6. **For custom/personalized stickers** (names, ship name, sail date): make a version with the
   text area empty for Printify, plus a "sample" version (e.g. "The Smith's") for mockups only.
   This is the same rule as the Anniversary shirt: never bake a sample name into the print file.

---

## 4. Clean up in Photopea (free, photopea.com)

Use Photopea for the things Canva does badly:

1. **Open** the Canva PNG (File → Open).
2. **Check the transparency.** You should see the gray/white checkerboard behind the art.
   Remove any leftover white box with the Magic Wand → Delete.
3. **Add a proper sticker outline:** Layer → Layer Style → **Stroke**:
   - Size **25–40 px**, Position **Outside**, Color **white**
   - This gives the classic die-cut sticker border Etsy buyers expect.
4. **Rounder outline (optional):** Select → Load selection from the layer, Select → Modify →
   **Expand 30 px**, then **Smooth 15 px**. Fill white on a new layer below the art.
5. **Mockups (optional):** File → Open a free sticker mockup PSD (laptop, tumbler, water bottle),
   double-click the smart-object layer, paste your sticker, and save.
   (I can also make sticker mockups the way I did the shirts. Just send the PNGs.)
6. **Export:** File → Export As → **PNG** (keeps transparency).

---

## 5. Create the product in Printify

1. **Printify → Create product → search "Kiss-Cut Stickers"** → choose **Printify Choice**.
2. **Upload** the PNG and center it. It should fill the print area, inside the margin.
3. **Sizes:** enable **3" and 4"** to start (the most popular). Add 2" and 6" later.
4. **Personalized stickers:** add a **Personalization text layer** (like your Anniversary
   and Girls Trip shirts) where the name goes. Check the preview shows **one** name only.
5. **Mockups:** Printify's are fine to start. Replace them with custom ones later.
6. **Publish to Etsy**, and also **to Shopify** if you want the same product there.
   (When you publish again later, untick **Images** if you've uploaded custom photos to Etsy.)

---

## 6. Pricing (do this before you publish)

Stickers are cheap, so fees take a bigger share. Check the real **production + shipping cost**
in Printify for your size, then price around it:

- **Etsy fees:** $0.20 listing + **6.5%** transaction + payment processing (≈3% + $0.25 in the US).
- **Single stickers:** about **$4–$6** each. One guide's example has a Printify sticker costing
  about $1.39 to make and selling for $3.99–$4.99, but shipping is what decides your margin.
  Always check Printify's shipping cost.
- **Make the money on bundles:**
  - **"Pick any 3" / "Cruise pack of 5"** priced as one listing.
  - **Add-on to a shirt order.** Put the matching sticker in the shirt listing's
    "Match the whole crew" section.
- **Etsy's $35 free-shipping threshold.** A 5-sticker bundle plus a shirt pushes buyers over it.

## 7. List it on Etsy

Copy your shirt listing structure (`etsy-listing-seo.md`):

- **Title:** `Family Cruise Sticker 2027, Personalized Cruise Sticker, Matching Family Cruise Gift, Tumbler Sticker`
- **Tags (13):** cruise sticker, family cruise 2027, cruise tumbler decal, cruise door decor,
  girls trip sticker, cruise gift, personalized sticker, laptop sticker, water bottle sticker,
  cruise ship sticker, vacation sticker, cruise squad, matching cruise.
- **Personalization box:** "Enter the name exactly as you want it printed (max 15 characters)."
- **Photos (in this order):** the sticker on white → on a tumbler → size chart (2"/3"/4") →
  the matching shirt or door magnet → bundle photo.
- **Cross-link:** add the sticker to your door kit, magnet and shirt listings
  ("Match the whole crew"). I can add those links once the sticker listings are live.

## 8. Shopify (your second storefront)

1. Printify publishes the same sticker products to Shopify. No new design work.
2. **Put stickers in a "Cruise Add-Ons" collection** and show it on each shirt's product page
   ("Complete the set").
3. **Bundle discount:** a Shopify automatic discount such as *"Buy 3 stickers, get 15% off"*.
4. Prices can be a little lower than Etsy, since there are no Etsy fees. That's a reason for
   repeat buyers to come to your own site.

---

## 9. GoHighLevel (GHL): turn one-time buyers into repeat buyers

Connect GHL to your **Shopify** store (GHL's Shopify integration). Etsy doesn't let you market to
Etsy buyers by email, so Shopify and social traffic are where GHL works.

Set up these 4 automations:

1. **Opt-in.** A pop-up or form: *"Get a free printable cruise door sign + 10% off stickers."*
   Send them the door-sign PDF you already have, so it costs you nothing.
2. **Abandoned cart.** Email at 1 hour and SMS at 24 hours, offering free shipping over $35.
3. **Post-purchase upsell.** 2 days after a shirt order: *"Add matching stickers before you
   sail."* Link to the sticker bundle.
4. **Cruise countdown.** Ask for the **sail date** at checkout or in the opt-in. Send reminders at
   60, 30 and 14 days before it with the newest designs, gift ideas for the group, and door decor.

## 11. Launch checklist

- [ ] Make 3 stickers in Canva at 2000 × 2000 PNG with a transparent background
- [ ] Add white outlines in Photopea and export PNG
- [ ] Create Kiss-Cut products in Printify (3" + 4"), adding personalization where needed
- [ ] Check Printify production and shipping costs, then set prices and a 3-pack bundle
- [ ] Publish to Etsy and Shopify
- [ ] Add "Match the whole crew" sticker links to the shirt, magnet and door kit listings
- [ ] Set up the GHL opt-in, abandoned-cart and post-purchase flows
- [ ] Add 5 more designs per week. Sticker shops grow with catalog size.
