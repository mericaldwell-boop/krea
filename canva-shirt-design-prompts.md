# Canva AI Shirt Design Prompts: Sail & Seek Studio

## Read this first: what Canva can and can't control
Canva's image generator **decides its own output size**, usually about 1–1.5K pixels. You can't set it to 4500 px or 300 DPI in the prompt, and it usually adds a background. Writing "4500 x 5400" into the prompt won't change the file. So, to avoid regenerating anything:

1. **Generate** with the prompts below. They're written to give a clean, centered design on a plain white background, which is the easiest kind to cut out.
2. **Remove the background:** click the image → **Edit** → **BG Remover**.
3. **Upscale:** click the image → **Edit** → **Upscale** (or "Enhance"). Use the highest option.
4. **Put it on a print-size page:** create a **custom design 4500 x 5400 px**, paste the design in, and make it about **4000 px wide**, centered, near the top.
5. **Export:** **Share → Download → PNG**, with **"Transparent background"** checked and size at **1x (4500 x 5400)**.

That gives you a **4500 x 5400 px transparent PNG**, which is exactly what Printify wants for a Bella+Canvas 3001 front print (15" x 18" at 300 DPI).

**Text tip:** AI often misspells words. Zoom in and check every letter before you upscale. If a word is wrong, regenerate just that one, or generate it **without text** (delete the lettering lines from the prompt) and add the words yourself in Canva with a bold rounded font.

## Make your own variations
Swap the parts in brackets:
```
T-shirt print design, isolated on a plain solid white background, no shirt, no mockup, no border. Vintage screen-print cartoon illustration with slight distressed texture, bold clean navy outlines, bright flat colors. Center: [DESCRIBE THE SCENE]. Bold rounded retro lettering above in [COLOR] reading exactly "[TOP WORDS]", below in [COLOR] reading exactly "[BOTTOM WORDS]". Palette: navy, coral, teal, sunny yellow, white. Centered composition, square format, high detail.
```
- **For personalization:** add *"At the bottom, a completely EMPTY blank ribbon banner with absolutely no text on it."* Then add the name as a text layer in Printify.
- **For dark shirts:** change the lettering colors to white and sunny yellow and say *"designed to print on a navy shirt."*

## Final checklist before uploading to Printify
- [ ] Every word spelled right (zoom in)
- [ ] Background removed, no white box left around the design
- [ ] Upscaled
- [ ] Placed on a 4500 x 5400 px page, about 4000 px wide
- [ ] Downloaded as PNG with a transparent background
